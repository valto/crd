# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive work on open capability documentation.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and evidence in good faith;
- distinguish facts, inferences, defaults, examples, and unknowns;
- respect contributors and their time;
- avoid discriminatory, harassing, or abusive behaviour; and
- keep discussion focused on the project and its users.

The maintainer may remove contributions or restrict participation when conduct undermines these expectations.
