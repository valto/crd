Capability Inventory

A discoverable catalogue of available or planned Capability MLEs. It links to CRDs when they exist but does not require a Product Requirements Document above it.

View Markdown source ↗

Purpose

An inventory makes reusable and planned capabilities visible across products, services, agents, and applications. A CRD may be linked from a product or PRD, but it can also stand alone in an open inventory.

Independent reuse

Schedule a meeting can have one reusable CRD and be realized differently by many products. The inventory makes that common capability discoverable without making any one product’s PRD its owner.
