A CRD is like a feature specification for the agentic era.

It is smaller than a PRD and more rigorous than a typical feature definition: it specifies one complete capability’s purpose, boundaries, requirements, interaction contracts, realizations, evidence, and unknowns—so people, agents, and software can work from the same durable meaning.

Capability Documentation is the open framework. A capability may belong to a product, service, system, agent, or reusable open inventory; its Capability Requirements Document (CRD) makes purpose, boundaries, requirements, interaction contracts, and realizations understandable before that meaning fragments across UX, code, APIs, agents, and operations.

What

A requirement specification for a complete capability.

A CRD describes the smallest complete, contextually meaningful ability that produces an outcome—not merely a feature, endpoint, component, or function.

For whom

People, agents, and software.

The framework gives product, domain, UX, and engineering people—as well as AI agents and applications—a shared language for understanding and using capabilities.

Why

Keep meaning from being lost.

It separates purpose, rules, defaults, examples, implementation choices, operational constraints, and unknowns so they are not silently confused or discarded.

How

Connect requirements to execution.

Define a Capability MLE, its Interaction Contract MLEs, and one or more realizations—then trace the CRD to the relevant UX, APIs, tools, code, tests, and operations.

Use with your AI agent

Give your agent the CRD context once.

Copy this prompt into your AI agent. It directs the agent to the CRD source material, lets it answer questions for its owner, and gives it a disciplined way to begin drafting CRDs from product, design, code, and service evidence.

The agent is instructed to preserve unknowns, distinguish facts from inference, and not change a live service without explicit approval.

Origins

Why Minimum Logical Element matters.

MLE is the scope discipline beneath CRD: the smallest bundled unit that still retains logical sense and context across business, design, and development. It prevents both vague feature buckets and isolated technical fragments.

A Capability MLE is the complete-ability form of that idea; a CRD is its durable requirement specification.

Why it exists

Shared meaning across product, software, and agents.

CRD keeps general capability purpose separate from organization-specific rationale and implementation. It makes rules, defaults, examples, operational constraints, inferences, and unknowns explicit—so current code is not mistaken for the fundamental requirement.
